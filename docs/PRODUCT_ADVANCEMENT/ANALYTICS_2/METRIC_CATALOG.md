# Metric Catalog — Analytics 2.0

**Module:** `packages/platform-core/src/analytics-2-0.mjs` (`METRIC_CATALOG_V2`)

Defines 12 canonical metrics across all 10 business domains (`crm`, `sales`, `financial`, `scheduling`, `communication`, `workflow`, `integration`, `ai`, `marketplace`, `saas`) with explicit `sourceTable`, `aggregation`, `dateField`, `currencyMode`, `permissions`, and `freshness`.
