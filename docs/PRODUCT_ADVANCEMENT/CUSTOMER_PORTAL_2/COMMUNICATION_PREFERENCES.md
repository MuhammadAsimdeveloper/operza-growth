# Customer Portal 2.0 — Communication Preferences & Consent

- **Preference Center:** Integrates with `communication_consents` and `engagement_suppression_list` across Email, SMS, and WhatsApp.
- **Immediate Opt-Out:** Revoking consent in the portal takes effect immediately for future campaigns and Workflow 2.0 journey steps (`evaluateRecipientEligibility`).
- **Category Separation:** Essential account-security and transactional notifications remain separated from marketing consent boundaries; no automatic legal compliance certification is claimed.
